SwipeSkip scrolls YouTube Shorts for you, on a timer you choose, during a session you start.

• Intervals from 3 to 120 seconds, session limits, optional scroll cap
• Always-visible Stop button in a notification
• Pauses when you touch the screen, open comments, or an ad is likely showing
• Diagnostics screen to find out why something does not work
• Free, open source, no ads, no analytics, no network permission

It never taps ads or the Skip button, never likes, comments or subscribes, and never reads or stores screen text. Uses the Android accessibility service; the in-app setup explains exactly what that means.

Not affiliated with or endorsed by YouTube or Google. Developed with AI assistance and human review.
